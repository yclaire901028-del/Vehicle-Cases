Localizing Electric Truck Production in Belarus

Shentou Mobility and its strategic partners are advancing localized electric truck production in the EAEU market. A full week of continuous winter road tests was conducted in Minsk under extreme −15°C conditions.

Extreme Cold-Region Endurance

The electric truck was evaluated in urban and medium-distance logistics. Operating at a gross vehicle weight of 4.5 to 7.5 tons with a 2-ton actual payload, the vehicle maintained an average operating speed of 70 km/h with no significant performance degradation observed.

Exceptional Energy Efficiency

Test results demonstrated exceptional energy efficiency and low-temperature endurance. The truck achieved a stable driving range of approximately 250 km while operating between 100% and 20% state of charge (SOC). Under extended full-load testing conditions, it reached a maximum range of nearly 290 km from 100% to 2% SOC, proving highly competitive charging stability.
